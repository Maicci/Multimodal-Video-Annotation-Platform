# Administrator guide

Open **Videos** to upload a clip, assign it to an active annotator, or check
processing and transcription errors. Use **Pending users** to review sign-up
requests and assign roles.

Open **Assignment progress** and choose **Video** or **Annotator** under
**Group by**. Each row counts all assignments for that video or person.
**Completed · ready to submit** means the annotator marked the work complete.
**Submitted · awaiting review** means a reviewer can act on it.
Use **Refresh progress** after an assignment or review decision.
